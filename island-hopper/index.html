Island Hopper

Lady Hush's fog has drained three islands grey. Each one hides a piece of an old sea chart. Win all three and the Sunken Lighthouse will rise from the deep.

  • SailA / D or ← / → to steer, W / S for speed. On a phone, use the round buttons.
  • PatrolsGrey Hushfleet ships guard the islands. Dodge them or fire your cannons (Space or FIRE) side-on.
  • Pip · LA gust that shoves patrols and shots away. Handy for escaping.
  • LandSail close to a grey island and press E or tap Land! Each island has a quick challenge for one of the crew.
  • HarboursPatrols won't follow you near Lantern Cove or an island you've cleared, and your hull mends there.
